FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Key information regarding our export locations, logistics, packaging, and order requirements.

We regularly export high-grade Pakistani fresh fruits and vegetables to Dubai (UAE), Qatar, Muscat (Oman), Sri Lanka, Malaysia, Singapore, and European ports. We can also arrange shipments to any international port with cold chain logistics connectivity.

Our Head Office is situated at Suite # 10th Floor, Land Mark Plaza, Opp. Jang Press, I.I. Chundrigar Road, Karachi – 74200. Our sorting, grading, and yard warehouse is located at Shop # 123, Onion & Potato Shed, New Sabzi Mandi, Karachi.

Yes! All our shipments comply with destination country plant quarantine regulations. We issue official Phytosanitary Certificates, Certificate of Origin, Form E, and Bill of Lading for all European and Asian shipments.

We utilize temperature-controlled pre-cooling facilities immediately after harvest. For Mangoes, Hot Water Treatment (HWT) or Vapour Heat Treatment (VHT) is applied when required. Shipments travel in pre-cooled 40ft High Cube Reefer containers at optimized temperature and humidity settings (e.g., +13°C for Mangoes, +4°C to +6°C for Kinnow).

Our standard sea freight MOQ is 1 x 40ft Reefer Container (approx. 24-28 Metric Tons depending on produce). For high-value fresh mangoes, air freight shipments start from 1 to 5 Tons. Packaging options include 5kg/10kg mesh bags, corrugated export cartons, telescopic boxes, or custom private label branding.
